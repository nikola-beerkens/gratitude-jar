# 🌸 Gratitude Jar 🌸

A cozy little pixel-style app for collecting daily moments of thankfulness, one glowing orb at a time.  
Write what you're grateful for, drop it into your magical jar, and watch your jar fill up with soft, colorful joy.

![Gratitude Jar Screenshot](screenshot.png)

---

## ✨ Features

- 🌈 Add daily gratitude entries as glowing pixel orbs
- 💖 See your jar slowly fill with positive thoughts
- 🌟 Tooltips or hover to revisit what each orb holds

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- HTML + CSS (pixel aesthetic magic)
- JavaScript (orb handling & entry management)
- LocalStorage (your memories, safe & local)

---

## 🌼 How to Use

1. Open the app
2. Type something you're grateful for (big or small!)  
3. Hit Enter or Submit — watch your orb pop into the jar ✨  
4. Hover or click to revisit old entries  
5. Come back tomorrow. Your jar loves to grow 🌱
